# Practice & Curricula

Broad, cross-tool curricula, exercise banks, sandboxes, and channels.

- **[90DaysOfDevOps](https://github.com/MichaelCade/90DaysOfDevOps)** — comprehensive day-by-day DevOps curriculum spanning Linux, networking, git, Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD, cloud, and IaC, with real written content.
- **[DevOps Toolkit](https://www.youtube.com/@DevOpsToolkit)** — well-regarded, continuously updated YouTube channel covering Kubernetes, GitOps, platform engineering, and cloud-native tooling.
- **[TechWorld with Nana](https://www.youtube.com/@TechWorldwithNana)** — hugely popular, approachable channel covering Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD, Git, and cloud fundamentals.
- **[TechnoTim](https://www.youtube.com/@TechnoTim)** — popular homelab/self-hosting channel covering Kubernetes, Docker, Proxmox, and networking.
- **[Marcel Dempers](https://www.youtube.com/@MarcelDempers)** — practical, in-depth video series on Docker, Kubernetes, cloud, and CI/CD.
- **[CNCF](https://www.youtube.com/@cncf)** — official channel with KubeCon/CloudNativeCon talks and project deep-dives across the cloud-native ecosystem.
- **[DevOps Exercises](https://github.com/bregman-arie/devops-exercises)** — large bank of practice questions and interview prep across Linux, networking, Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD, and Terraform.
- **[KodeKloud](https://kodekloud.com/)** — free browser-based playgrounds (no local setup) for hands-on practice with Kubernetes, Docker, Ansible, Terraform, and more; some courses are paywalled, playgrounds are free.
- **[Killercoda](https://killercoda.com/)** — free, guided scenario-based practice labs with step validation, across Kubernetes, Docker, Linux, Terraform, and more.
